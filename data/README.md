# Data

Data files are **not** included in this repository and are not redistributable.

## Source

Osteoporosis arm of the matched case-control study published as:

> Usala RL, Fernandez SJ, Mete M, Cowen L, Shara NM, Barsony J, Verbalis JG.
> Hyponatremia Is Associated With Increased Osteoporosis and Bone Fractures
> in a Large US Health System Population.
> *J Clin Endocrinol Metab*. 2015;100(8):3021-3031.
> doi:10.1210/jc.2015-1261

Extracted from the MedStar Health system electronic health records.
Cases (n=30,517) matched 1:1 with controls on age, sex, race, and
patient record length.

## Expected local files

Place locally, do not commit:

- `osteo_impute.csv` — 5 stacked imputations, `_Imputation_` index column
- `osteo_clean.csv` — cleaned single-imputation extract

Data are held under the original study's institutional agreements and are
not available from this repository. Direct inquiries to the corresponding
